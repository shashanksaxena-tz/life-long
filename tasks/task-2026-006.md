## Variants
- **Primary**: Solid fill, high emphasis
- **Secondary**: Outline, medium emphasis
- **Ghost**: No background, low emphasis
- **Destructive**: Red, for dangerous actions

## Sizes
- `sm` (28px), `md` (36px), `lg` (44px)

## States
All variants support: default, hover, active, focus, disabled, loading
