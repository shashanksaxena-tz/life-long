## Problem
Refresh tokens were expiring after 15 minutes instead of 7 days due to a misconfigured TTL in the Redis store.

## Root Cause
The `REFRESH_TOKEN_TTL` env var was being parsed as **minutes** instead of **seconds**. The value `604800` (7 days in seconds) was treated as 604800 minutes.

## Fix
- Updated `tokenService.js` to explicitly use seconds: `redis.setex(key, ttl, token)`
- Added unit test for TTL validation
- Added monitoring alert for tokens expiring < 1 hour after issuance
