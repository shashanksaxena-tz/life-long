## Token System
Created a comprehensive token system with 12-step color scales.

### Color Scales
- **Gray**: 12 steps from #09090b to #fafafa
- **Blue**: 12 steps from #0c1929 to #eff6ff
- **Green**: 12 steps from #052e16 to #f0fdf4
- **Red**: 12 steps from #450a0a to #fef2f2

### Semantic Tokens
- `bg-primary`, `bg-secondary`, `bg-tertiary`
- `text-primary`, `text-secondary`, `text-muted`
- `border-primary`, `border-secondary`
- `accent-*` for each hue
