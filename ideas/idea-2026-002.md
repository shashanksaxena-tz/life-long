## Self-Healing Infrastructure

Automate common failure recovery patterns:

1. Auto-restart crashed services with health check validation
2. Auto-scale based on queue depth, not just CPU
3. Automated rollback on error rate spike
4. Self-repairing database connections

Could integrate with PagerDuty for escalation when auto-recovery fails.
