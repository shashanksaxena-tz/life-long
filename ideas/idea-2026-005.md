## Gesture-Based Task Management

Natural gesture shortcuts for mobile app:

- **Swipe right**: Mark task as done
- **Swipe left**: Snooze to tomorrow
- **Long press + drag**: Reorder priorities
- **Pinch**: Zoom between day/week/month views
- **Shake**: Undo last action

Inspired by Tinder's swipe UX and Clear app's gesture-first approach.
