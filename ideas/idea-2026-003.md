## Collaborative Workspaces

Add real-time collaboration features:

- **Shared projects** with role-based permissions
- **Live cursors** showing who's editing what
- **Comments and threads** on tasks
- **@mentions** with notification routing

Could use Y.js or Automerge for CRDT-based real-time sync.
