## Personal Knowledge Graph

Build a knowledge graph connecting all entities:

- Tasks linked to projects, people, and concepts
- Auto-extract entities from log entries and notes
- Graph visualization for exploring connections
- Search that understands relationships ("tasks related to authentication")

Could use Neo4j or a simpler adjacency list in PostgreSQL with recursive CTEs.
